# European Green Deal + Adaptation Strategy

## 1. 문서 정체성

EU 27개국이 채택한 단일 시장 차원의 기후 정책 패키지. Green Deal (2019) → Climate Law (2021, NDC 55% by 2030) → Adaptation Strategy (2021) → Fit for 55 입법 패키지 (2021-2024).

## 2. EU Adaptation Strategy (2021) 핵심

- 4개 priority: smarter, faster, more systemic, international action
- Climate-resilience as legal duty (EU Climate Law Article 5)
- Climate Adapt platform (EEA 운영) — best practices repository
- Insurance gap closing (insurance penetration 35% → target)

## 3. NATO 4-axis 사용 패턴

| Axis | 사용 빈도 | 예시 |
