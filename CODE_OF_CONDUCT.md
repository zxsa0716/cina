# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment for academic
collaboration on climate-negotiation analytics, we as contributors and the
maintainer pledge to make participation in CINA a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to creating a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, especially academic critique
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback on methodology or claims
- Focusing on what is best for the research community
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments and personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Bad-faith framing of climate-policy positions or country negotiating stances
  (CINA is a research tool — countries' stances should be discussed
  empirically, not polemically)
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Academic-specific principles

CINA is a graduate research project. We additionally commit to:

- **Honest framing**: claims are framed accurately (e.g., "single-case finding",
  "simulated panel"), without inflation
- **Transparent disagreement**: methodology critique is welcome and answered
  with literature, not defensiveness
- **No partisan framing of negotiation positions**: countries' stances are
  research targets, not political endorsements

## Our Responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behaviour and is expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any instances of unacceptable behaviour.

The maintainer has the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when
an individual is representing CINA.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported by contacting the maintainer at **zxsa0716@kookmin.ac.kr**. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response
that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
