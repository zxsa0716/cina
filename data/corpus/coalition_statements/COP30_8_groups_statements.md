# COP30 협상 그룹 8개 statements

## 1. G77+China (Iraq presidency)

**회원국**: 134개국 + China (gross majority of developing countries)

**핵심 발화 (opening, 2025-11-10)**:
> "We reaffirm the principles of equity and common but differentiated responsibilities and respective capabilities. Climate finance commitments from developed country Parties must be honoured in full, in addition to the scaling-up of adaptation finance. The Loss and Damage Fund operationalization remains paramount."

**입장 분포**:
- 적응재원 doubling: support strongly
- L&D Fund operationalization: support strongly
- GGA indicators: support voluntary nature (consensus with chair Brazil)
- mitigation new commitments for developing countries: oppose

**stance signal**: development frame + justice frame mix

---

## 2. AOSIS (Antigua and Barbuda chair)

**회원국**: 39개국 (Maldives, Tuvalu, Marshall Is, Fiji, Vanuatu, Caribbean states 등)

**핵심 발화**:
> "For Small Island Developing States, 1.5°C is not a target — it is a survival line. The triple finance decision is necessary but not sufficient. We need accelerated indicator implementation aligned with the 1.5°C pathway, not delayed by procedural caveats."

**입장**:
- 1.5°C alignment 강조
- L&D Fund 자금 흐름 가속화
- GGA indicators의 ambition gap 비판 (norm entrepreneur)

**stance signal**: justice frame dominant + security frame

**Norm Entrepreneur 역할** (Finnemore & Sikkink 1998):
- 1991년부터 30년간 일관된 norm advocacy
- L&D Fund 신설의 doctrinal author
- 1.5°C pathway의 정치적 정착

---

## 3. AILAC (Costa Rica chair, COP30 cycle)

**회원국**: 8개국 (Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras, Panama, Paraguay, Peru)

**핵심 발화**:
> "AILAC welcomes the GGA indicator framework as a critical step. We call for these indicators to be implemented in a manner that maintains scientific integrity and policy ambition, bridging the divide between developed and developing country Parties through cooperative action."

**입장**: norm entrepreneur bridge role (G77 — Annex I)
- 1.5°C 강조
- mitigation ambition 옹호
- adaptation cooperation

**stance signal**: development frame + scientific frame

---

## 4. EIG (Switzerland chair, COP30 cycle)

**회원국**: 6개국 (Switzerland, Mexico, Korea, Norway, Liechtenstein, Monaco, Georgia)

**핵심 발화**:
> "The Environmental Integrity Group supports a balanced GGA indicator framework that respects national circumstances while maintaining scientific integrity. We emphasize the importance of robust, transparent reporting under national adaptation plans as a foundation for global progress."

**한국 발화 비중**: ~25% (NAP framework 강조)

**입장**: middle ground bridge
- voluntary nature acknowledge
- national reporting/transparency 강조
- ambition + flexibility balance

**stance signal**: development + scientific frame

---

## 5. HAC (High Ambition Coalition — Marshall Is + EU + UK lead)

**회원국**: 80+ 국가 (Marshall Is leadership, EU, UK, AOSIS members, LDCs, AILAC core)

**핵심 발화**:
> "The High Ambition Coalition urges the most ambitious possible interpretation of the GGA framework and the triple finance commitment. We cannot allow procedural compromises to undermine the urgency of the climate crisis."

**입장**:
- 1.5°C alignment 강조
- adaptation finance 적극 옹호
- L&D Fund full operationalization

---

## 6. Umbrella Group (USA, Canada, Australia, Japan, NZ, Norway, Iceland 등)

**핵심 발화**:
> "The Umbrella Group supports the operationalization of the Global Goal on Adaptation through voluntary, country-driven indicators that respect the diverse capacities of all Parties. Finance contributions should reflect realistic donor capabilities and innovative finance instruments."

**입장**:
- voluntary nature 강조 (의무화 회피)
- contributor base 확대 옹호
- innovative finance source 강조 (MDB, private)

**USA Trump-era 영향**:
- 본 statement는 outgoing Biden administration leadership 하에서 작성
- 2025년 1월 Trump 2기 출범 후 USA의 active engagement는 감소

---

## 7. AGN (African Group of Negotiators, Kenya chair)

**회원국**: 54개 아프리카 국가

**핵심 발화**:
> "The African Group calls for the triple finance decision to be fully realized through public finance, not predominantly through private and innovative sources. Loss and Damage support must reach the most vulnerable in Africa, particularly drought-affected and conflict-affected communities."

**입장**:
- 적응재원 public source 강조 (private source 회의)
- L&D Fund Africa 접근 우선화
- 기술이전 + capacity building

**stance signal**: justice frame + development frame

---

## 8. LMDC (Like-Minded Developing Countries, Bolivia chair 2025)

**회원국**: ~25개국 (China, India, Saudi Arabia, Bolivia, Ecuador, Vietnam, Sri Lanka, Cuba 등)

**핵심 발화**:
> "LMDC reaffirms the equity-based architecture of the climate regime. The triple finance must be additional to existing ODA commitments. We oppose any expansion of the contributor base that would shift responsibility away from developed country Parties."

**입장**:
- CBDR-RC 강조
- contributor base 확대 강하게 반대
- mitigation new commitments 회피
- adaptation 재원 doubling은 minimum

**stance signal**: sovereignty frame + justice frame

## 9. 종합 분석 (Choi 2026 §4.2)

8개 그룹의 stance 분포는 Leiden community detection으로 2개 cluster로 분할:

**C0 (development-frame consistent)**: Brazil (chair), EU, EIG, AILAC bridge, AGN bridge
**C1 (justice/sovereignty mix)**: AOSIS, LMDC, India, Saudi, China
