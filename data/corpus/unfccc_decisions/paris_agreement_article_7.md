# Paris Agreement Article 7 — Adaptation (treaty text)

## 1. 문서 정체성

기후변화 적응의 헌법적 기반. UNFCCC 산하 Paris 협정의 14개 조항 중 적응 영역을 단독으로 다루는 유일한 조항. CINA 모든 분석의 normative 기준점.

## 2. 핵심 조항 전문

### Article 7.1 — Global Goal on Adaptation 설정
"Parties hereby establish the global goal on adaptation of enhancing adaptive capacity, strengthening resilience and reducing vulnerability to climate change, with a view to contributing to sustainable development and ensuring an adequate adaptation response in the context of the temperature goal referred to in Article 2."

### Article 7.5 — 국가 적응 노력의 인정
"Parties acknowledge that adaptation action should follow a country-driven, gender-responsive, participatory and fully transparent approach, taking into consideration vulnerable groups, communities and ecosystems."

### Article 7.7 — 국제 협력
"Parties should strengthen their cooperation on enhancing action on adaptation, taking into account the Cancun Adaptation Framework, including with regard to:
(a) Sharing information, good practices, experiences and lessons learned;
(b) Strengthening institutional arrangements;
(c) Strengthening scientific knowledge;
(d) Assisting developing country Parties in identifying effective adaptation practices..."

### Article 7.10-13 — 적응 communications
"Each Party should, as appropriate, submit and update periodically an adaptation communication."

### Article 7.14 — Global Stocktake
"The global stocktake referred to in Article 14 shall, inter alia:
(a) Recognize adaptation efforts of developing country Parties;
(b) Enhance the implementation of adaptation action;
(c) Review the adequacy and effectiveness of adaptation and support provided for adaptation;
(d) Review the overall progress made in achieving the global goal on adaptation."

## 3. GGA의 헌법적 위치

Article 7.1의 "global goal on adaptation"은 COP21(2015)부터 운영적 정의가 부재했다. COP21 → COP26 → COP28 → COP30의 10년에 걸친 운영화 과정:
- COP21 (2015): GGA 헌법적 선언만
- COP26 (2021): Glasgow-Sharm 2-year work programme 신설
- COP27 (2022): framework discussion 진전
- COP28 (2023): UAE 7개 thematic targets framework 채택
- COP30 (2025): 59개 voluntary indicators 채택 (L.25E)

이는 IR이론적으로 **regime maturation through chair-relay** 패턴 — 5개 의장국이 동일 framework의 정교화를 단계적으로 진행.

## 4. CINA records와의 normative 연결

v5의 모든 GGA-IND, GGA-MOI, NAPs records는 본 조항의 운영화 진전을 측정. 본 조항이 모든 GGA 관련 stance의 normative anchor.
