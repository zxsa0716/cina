# 추가 17개국 적응정책 brief

본 문서는 KOR / BRA / USA / EU 4개국을 제외한 v5의 핵심 17개국에 대한 간략한 정책 brief 모음. 각 country brief에는 (a) 주요 적응 정책 문서, (b) NATO 4축 사용 패턴, (c) 협상 블록 정합도, (d) CINA stance 요약 포함.

---

## 1. China (CHN)

- **NDC 2025 update**: 2035년 NDC, 1차 emission peak 2030 이전
- **National Climate Adaptation Strategy 2035** (2022 발표)
- 적응재원: 국내 fiscal 위주, 외부 ODA 거의 받지 않음
- 협상: G77+China leader, LMDC core
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND -0.30 (mitigation linkage 회의), L&D-OP +0.65 (historical responsibility), TECH-TRANS +0.85

## 2. India (IND)

- **National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC)**: 8개 missions
- **State Action Plans on Climate Change (SAPCC)**: 32개 주
- 적응재원: GCF/AF 적극 활용, 국내 fiscal 보완
- 협상: G77+China, BASIC, LMDC, vulnerable group bridge
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND -0.15 (CBDR-RC 강조), L&D-OP +0.85, TECH-TRANS +0.92

## 3. South Africa (ZAF)

- **National Climate Change Adaptation Strategy 2020-2030**
- **JET-IP**: Just Energy Transition Investment Plan (USD 8.5bn pledged by UK/US/EU/Germany/France)
- 협상: G77, BASIC, AGN bridge
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.05, L&D-OP +0.75, JT-ADAPT +0.55

## 4. Indonesia (IDN)

- **Long-Term Strategy for Low Carbon and Climate Resilience 2050**
- 협상: G77, ASEAN
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND -0.10, L&D-OP +0.75, TECH-TRANS +0.78

## 5. Saudi Arabia (SAU)

- **Saudi Green Initiative**: 4 billion trees by 2030, renewable 50% by 2030
- 적응 강조보다 mitigation 비용 부담 회피 전략
- 협상: G77, Arab Group, OPEC
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND -0.55, L&D-OP +0.10, FINANCE-ADAPT +0.40 (defensive)

## 6. UAE (ARE)

- **UAE Net Zero by 2050 Strategic Initiative** (2021)
- COP28 의장국으로서 UAE Consensus 채택 (transition away from fossil fuels)
- USD 100M FRLD pledge
- 협상: G77, Arab Group, chair (COP28)
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.55, FINANCE-ADAPT +0.55 (COP28 host 효과 잔존)

## 7. Egypt (EGY)

- **National Climate Change Strategy 2050**
- COP27 의장국으로서 L&D Fund 신설 결정
- 협상: G77, Arab Group, AGN
- CINA stance: COP27 chair 효과 +0.04, L&D-OP +0.70

## 8. AOSIS (Small Island Developing States)

- 39개 회원국 (Tuvalu, Marshall Is, Maldives, AOSIS 등)
- 1.5°C alignment 핵심 norm entrepreneur
- L&D Fund 30년 캠페인의 leader (Finnemore & Sikkink 1998 norm cascade)
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.85, L&D-OP +0.95, FINANCE-ADAPT +0.92

## 9. AILAC (Independent Association of LatAm and Caribbean)

- 8개국: Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras, Panama, Paraguay, Peru
- Norm entrepreneur role between G77 and Annex I
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.85, JT-ADAPT +0.85

## 10. AGN (African Group of Negotiators)

- 54개 아프리카 회원국
- 적응 자금 + L&D + 기술이전 우선
- 협상: G77 sub-group
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.65, L&D-OP +0.75

## 11. LMDC (Like-Minded Developing Countries)

- ~25개국 (India, China, Saudi Arabia, Bolivia, Ecuador, Vietnam 등)
- CBDR-RC 강조, mitigation 부담 회피
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND -0.20, L&D-OP +0.55

## 12. Japan (JPN)

- **Climate Change Adaptation Plan** (2018, 5년 단위 갱신)
- ODA 적응 component 강화
- 협상: Umbrella Group
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.45, FINANCE-ADAPT +0.48 (mid-range)

## 13. Canada (CAN)

- **National Adaptation Strategy** (2023)
- 협상: Umbrella Group
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.45, L&D-OP +0.20 (cautious)

## 14. Australia (AUS)

- **National Climate Resilience and Adaptation Strategy 2021-2025**
- 석탄 수출 의존, 적응재원 보수적
- 협상: Umbrella Group
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.40, L&D-OP +0.10

## 15. UK (GBR)

- **3rd National Adaptation Programme 2023-2028**
- COP26 의장국으로서 Glasgow Pact 채택
- 협상: Umbrella Group (EU 탈퇴 후)
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.65, L&D-OP +0.55

## 16. Norway (NOR)

- **NDC + Long-Term Strategy** (석유 수출국이나 climate-progressive)
- 협상: EIG (Switzerland와 alignment)
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.70, FINANCE-ADAPT +0.82

## 17. Switzerland (CHE)

- **Swiss Climate Adaptation Strategy 2024-2030** (Choi 2026 §4.4 비교 대상)
- 협상: EIG leader
- CINA stance (COP30): GGA-IND +0.72, FINANCE-ADAPT +0.78
- 한국과 cosine similarity 0.78 (가장 가까운 EIG 동료국)
